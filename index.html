Privacy Policy — Ben'sFocusZone

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Ben'sFocusZone ("the Extension") is a personal productivity Chrome Extension. We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency regarding how your data is stored and synchronized.

1. Data We Access & Collect

  • Local Workspace Data: Your study tasks (To-Do list), time-blocking calendar entries, deadlines, Pomodoro preferences, custom quick links, YouTube playlist URLs, and theme settings are stored locally in your browser using localStorage and chrome.storage.
  • Google Account Profile (Optional): If you choose to click "Sign in with Google", the Extension requests basic profile scopes (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile) solely to display your name, email address, and profile avatar inside the Extension's Settings panel.
  • Google Drive File Access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): When signed in, the Extension uses the narrow drive.file scope exclusively to create and update a backup folder (Ben'sFocusZone Backups) containing your workspace snapshot JSON file (focustab_latest_backup.json) inside your personal Google Drive so you can restore your settings across devices or after reinstalling. The Extension cannot read, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure):
Ben'sFocusZone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. How We Use & Share Your Information

  • Zero Third-Party Selling or Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your Google user data or workspace data to any third parties, advertisers, or external analytics servers.
  • Direct Browser-to-Google Communication: All cloud backup transfers occur directly between your local Chrome browser and Google's official OAuth2 / Google Drive APIs over encrypted HTTPS. We do not operate any external middleman servers that store your personal data.

3. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

  • You can sign out at any time using the Sign Out button in Dashboard Settings → Personal, which immediately revokes and clears local authentication tokens.
  • You have full ownership of your backup files located in your Google Drive (Ben'sFocusZone Backups) and may delete them or revoke app access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
